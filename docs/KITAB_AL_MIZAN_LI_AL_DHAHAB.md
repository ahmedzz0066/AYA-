# كتاب الميزان للذهب
# Kitāb al-Mīzān li-l-Dhahab
### *The Book of the Balance for Gold*
#### Being a Chapter of al-Jabr wa-l-Muqābala Applied to the Market of XAUUSD

---

## I. Opening Statement

In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate.

Said Abū Jaʿfar Muḥammad ibn Mūsā al-Khwārizmī: When I composed the Book of Restoration
and Balancing, I observed that men were confounded not because the truth was hidden, but
because they had not **classified** their problems. They mixed the six forms of equation into
one heap and were then astonished that no single method served them. So I separated the forms,
and to each form I gave one algorithm, and to each algorithm one proof, and the confusion
ended.

I find the traders of gold in precisely this condition. They speak of "levels" as the
astrologers spoke of influences — by memory, by folklore, by the counting of touches. They draw
a line where price *stopped* and call it a cause, when it is only a shadow of a cause. They
have not asked the question that must be asked first:

> **What object, physically present in the book of orders, causes price to return?**

The answer is one thing and one thing only: **unfilled institutional intent** — an imbalance
created when one side transacted with such aggression that the opposing side was consumed before
it could be filled. The residue of that unfinished business is the zone. Everything else is
decoration.

I shall therefore classify, restore, balance, and then set down the algorithm complete, so that
a student may execute it without ambiguity, and a machine may execute it without a student.

## II. Classification of the Problem — The Six Forms

As the equation reduces to six canonical forms, so does the zone. Every mark a trader places on
the chart of gold belongs to one of these six and no seventh exists.

| Form | Name | Composition | Nature | Verdict |
|------|------|-------------|--------|---------|
| **I** | **Origination Demand / Supply** | Drop‑Base‑Rally (DBR); Rally‑Base‑Drop (RBD) | Reversal origin: the leg **into** the base opposes the leg **out of** it. The base is where the aggressor absorbed and turned the market. | **Highest form.** Admitted. |
| **II** | **Continuation Demand / Supply** | Rally‑Base‑Rally (RBR); Drop‑Base‑Drop (DBD) | Refill origin: unfilled remainder of an order already in motion, parked mid‑trend. | **Admitted**, subordinate to Form I, but supreme when aligned with HTF trend. |
| **III** | **Sweep‑Origin Zone** | Liquidity raid → base → displacement | Form I or II whose base is built **on the far side of a swept swing or equal high/low**. The aggressor manufactured its own counterparty before acting. | **The perfected form.** Ranked above all. |
| **IV** | **Naked Imbalance (FVG / gap only)** | Three‑candle inefficiency with **no base** | A true void, but with no resting order behind it. It is a *magnet*, not a *wall*. | **Not a zone.** Admitted only as a **target**, never as an entry. |
| **V** | **Compound / Nested Zone** | A lower‑timeframe zone lying inside a higher‑timeframe zone | Two orders at one price. Precision of the small, weight of the large. | **Admitted with elevated rank** (the confluence multiplier). |
| **VI** | **Counterfeit Zone** | Any base whose departure fails the magnitude gate; any zone twice consumed; any consolidation inside the Asian range interior | The appearance of the form without the substance. It is the "root without a square" — a term that cancels. | **Rejected absolutely.** Erase it. |

**The Prime Classification of the Session.** Beyond the form of the zone, the day itself has
three states, and a zone must be read in the state that bore it (all times **UTC**):

1. **Ḥālat al‑Jamʿ — the State of Accumulation** (23:00–06:00, Asian). Range is built. Zones born
   *inside* this range are Form VI. Only its **extremes** matter, as bait.
2. **Ḥālat al‑Khidāʿ — the State of Deception** (07:00–10:00, London "Judas"). The Asian extreme
   is swept, the crowd is trapped, and the true zone of the day is manufactured. **This is where
   Form III is born.**
3. **Ḥālat al‑Taṣdīq — the State of Confirmation** (12:30–16:00, New York). Either the London
   intent is continued (trade its zones) or the London extreme is itself swept and reversed
   (a second Form III).

## III. Restoration (al‑Jabr) — Stripping the Noise

Restoration is the removal of what has been subtracted falsely, and the discarding of terms that
do not belong to the equation. I therefore strike out the following from the science of gold:

1. **Touch‑counting.** "A level is stronger the more it is touched" — this is inverted. Each
   touch **consumes** resting orders. A zone touched twice is a spent cartridge. **Freshness is
   the axiom; repetition is decay.**
2. **The line.** A price is a scalar; an order is an interval. Draw rectangles or draw nothing.
3. **Round numbers as levels.** 2000.00 is not a wall. It is a **magazine of stop orders** — a
   place where liquidity is stored so that it may be taken. It has value only as a *modifier* of
   a real zone, never as a zone itself.
4. **Indicator confirmation** (RSI divergence, MACD crosses, stochastics). These are functions of
   past price re‑expressed; they add no new information to the equation. They are the same term
   written twice, and by al‑muqābala they cancel.
5. **The unweighted "order block."** To mark every last opposing candle is to mark the whole
   chart. Without a **magnitude gate on the departure**, the set is too large and expectancy is
   diluted to the spread.
6. **Trading inside the Asian range.** Gold in accumulation is a machine for manufacturing losses.
7. **Prediction of the news.** The scheduled release is not a market state; it is a suspension of
   the market's laws. Stand aside — 15 minutes before, 15 minutes after.

What remains after restoration is exactly four measurable things: **displacement, base,
imbalance, and freshness.** Everything admitted below is a function of these four.

## IV. Balancing (al‑Muqābala) — The Reduction to a Single Object

Set like against like. Displacement without a base is Form IV (a target). A base without
displacement is Form VI (noise). Displacement plus base plus imbalance, untouched, is the object
itself. Therefore:

> **Definition (The Zone).** Let a *base* B be a run of 1–5 candles of contracted range. Let the
> *departure* D be the run of ≤ 3 candles immediately following B whose net signed traversal
> exceeds a fixed multiple of the prevailing ATR, whose bodies dominate their ranges, and which
> leaves at least one three‑candle inefficiency (FVG). Then the rectangle
> `[distal, proximal]` spanned by B is a **true zone** of the polarity of D, and its rank is a
> function of (a) the strength of D, (b) the tightness of B, (c) the width of the FVG, (d) whether
> B was built upon swept liquidity, (e) higher‑timeframe agreement, (f) freshness, (g) proximity
> to a psychological magazine, and (h) the session that bore it.

Everything below is the mechanical execution of this one sentence.

## V. The Complete Algorithm — *al‑Khwārizmī's Twelve Steps for Gold*

**Instruments of measure.** Timeframes: **D1 & H4** for bias; **M15** for construction of zones
(*the primary tafsīr*); **M5** for refinement; **M1** for the trigger only. All times **UTC**.
Let `A = ATR(14)` on M15 (typically $2.50–$5.00 on gold), and `A_H4 = ATR(14)` on H4.

---

### **Step 0 — Prepare the Ledger (once per day, at 06:00 UTC)**

0.1 Compute `A` (M15 ATR‑14) and `A_D1` (daily ATR‑14). If `A_D1 < 60% of its 20‑day mean`, the
day is compressed: raise all magnitude gates by 15% and halve position size.
0.2 Mark the **Asian Range**: `AR_high`, `AR_low` over 23:00–06:00 UTC. Mark its midpoint.
0.3 Mark the **Previous Day** high, low, and close (`PDH`, `PDL`, `PDC`).
0.4 Mark the **psychological magazines** within `±2 × A_D1` of price, ranked:
    `×100.00` (weight 3) > `×50.00` (weight 2) > `×25.00 / ×10.00` (weight 1).
0.5 Mark the **news calendar**. Block windows of ±15 minutes around high‑impact USD events
    (CPI, NFP, FOMC, PCE, PPI, ISM). Inside a block window, the algorithm is halted.

---

### **Step 1 — Establish the Higher Bias (D1 → H4)**

1.1 On **D1**, determine structure by swing points: **bullish** if the last completed structure
    event is a close above the prior swing high (BOS); **bearish** if a close below the prior
    swing low; otherwise **neutral**.
1.2 Repeat on **H4**.
1.3 Set `BIAS`:
  - `BIAS = +1` (demand only) if D1 and H4 are both bullish, or one is bullish and the other neutral.
  - `BIAS = −1` (supply only) if both bearish, or one bearish and one neutral.
  - `BIAS = 0` (conflict) if D1 and H4 oppose. **In conflict, trade only Form III zones (sweep‑origin) and reduce size by half.**
1.4 On **H4**, mark the nearest **unmitigated** opposing zone above and below. These are the
    boundaries of the day's ambition — no target may be set beyond one of them.

---

### **Step 2 — Detect Candidate Bases (M15)**

For each index `i`, take the run `B = candles[j..k]` (length 1 to 5) and admit it as a base if:

2.1 **Height gate:** `high(B) − low(B) ≤ 0.60 × A`.
2.2 **Body gate:** the mean of `|close−open| / (high−low)` across `B` is `≤ 0.50`
    (a single‑candle base is admitted if its body ratio `≤ 0.50`, i.e. a doji/spinning top,
    **or** if its total range `≤ 0.35 × A`).
2.3 **Overlap gate:** every candle in `B` overlaps the first candle of `B` by at least 50% of its range.
2.4 A run that fails is not a base. Do not "adjust" it. It is Form VI.

---

### **Step 3 — Measure the Departure (the Magnitude Gate)**

Let `D` be the 1–3 candles immediately after `B`.

3.1 **Displacement gate:** `|close(last of D) − proximal(B)| ≥ 2.0 × A`.
3.2 **Conviction gate:** at least one candle of `D` has body/range `≥ 0.60`, and its opposing wick
    (the wick against the direction of travel) is `≤ 25%` of its range.
3.3 **Structure gate:** `D` must **close beyond** the most recent M15 swing point in its direction
    (a Break of Structure), not merely reach it.
3.4 **Imbalance gate (mandatory):** within `B ∪ D` there must exist a three‑candle FVG —
    for demand, `low(candle n+1) > high(candle n−1)`; for supply, `high(candle n+1) < low(candle n−1)` —
    of width `≥ 0.20 × A` (on gold, never accept less than **$0.60**).
3.5 If any of 3.1–3.4 fails, the base is **Form VI. Erase it.** This single step destroys more than
    80% of what retail traders mark, and that is its purpose.

---

### **Step 4 — Assign the Form and Draw the Rectangle**

4.1 Let `I` be the leg **into** `B` (the 1–3 candles before it) and `D` the leg out.
  - `I` down, `D` up → **DBR** (Form I, demand).
  - `I` up, `D` up → **RBR** (Form II, demand).
  - `I` up, `D` down → **RBD** (Form I, supply).
  - `I` down, `D` down → **DBD** (Form II, supply).
4.2 **Geometry — the two boundaries, defined without ambiguity:**
  - **Demand:** `distal = min(low of B)` (wicks included). `proximal_body = max(max(open,close) of B)`. `proximal_wick = max(high of B)`.
  - **Supply:** `distal = max(high of B)`. `proximal_body = min(min(open,close) of B)`. `proximal_wick = min(low of B)`.
  - **Entry is placed at `proximal_body`.** **Mitigation (a "tap") is counted at `proximal_wick`.**
    The first is the conservative price of business; the second is the honest price of contact.
    Confusing these two is the commonest error in the craft.
4.3 **Elevation to Form III:** if within the 12 candles preceding the departure the price traded
    beyond a prior swing extreme (or a pair of equal highs/lows within `0.15 × A`) **and then
    closed back inside**, the zone is **sweep‑origin**. Mark it `III`. It is the highest form.

---

### **Step 5 — Freshness (the Law of Decay)**

5.1 Count `taps` = the number of subsequent M15 candles that touched `proximal_wick` (high ≥ it
    for demand; low ≤ it for supply), counting **contiguous runs as one tap**.
5.2 `taps = 0` → **fresh** (full weight). `taps = 1` → **weakened** (partial weight, entry only
    with confirmation). `taps ≥ 2` → **dead. Erase the zone.**
5.3 Any **M15 close** beyond `distal` → the zone is **destroyed**, irrespective of taps. Erase it.

---

### **Step 6 — The Balance (al‑Mīzān): Scoring, 0–100**

Only zones passing every gate of Steps 2, 3, 5 reach the balance. Award:

| # | Criterion | Measure | Max |
|---|-----------|---------|-----|
| 1 | **Departure strength** | `min(20, 10 × (displacement / (2A)))` | 20 |
| 2 | **Base quality** | 15 if 1–2 candles and height ≤ 0.35A; 10 if 3 candles; 5 if 4–5 | 15 |
| 3 | **Imbalance width** | `min(15, 15 × (FVG width / (0.5A)))` | 15 |
| 4 | **Liquidity sweep (Form III)** | 15 if sweep‑origin; 7 if the departure merely broke structure with a wick raid; 0 otherwise | 15 |
| 5 | **HTF alignment** | 15 if zone polarity `= BIAS` and the zone sits inside or at an H4 zone; 10 if polarity `= BIAS`; 0 if `BIAS = 0`; **−25 if opposed to BIAS** | 15 |
| 6 | **Freshness** | 10 if `taps = 0`; 4 if `taps = 1` | 10 |
| 7 | **Psychological magazine** | 5 × (weight/3) if `proximal_body` lies within `0.25 × A` of a magazine | 5 |
| 8 | **Session of birth** | 5 if born 07:00–10:00 or 12:30–16:00 UTC; 2 if 06:00–07:00 or 10:00–12:30; **0 if born inside the Asian range interior** | 5 |

**Grades.**
- **Grade A: score ≥ 80** with all gates passed → *limit order permitted*.
- **Grade B: 65 ≤ score < 80** → *confirmation entry only* (Step 8.2).
- **Below 65 → not a trade.** There is no "almost." The equation either balances or it does not.

---

### **Step 7 — Selection of the Zone of the Day**

7.1 Retain only zones whose polarity equals `BIAS` (or, when `BIAS = 0`, only Form III).
7.2 Retain only zones **not yet reached** by price and lying within `1.5 × A_D1` of the current price.
7.3 Rank by score; on ties, prefer: Form III > Form I > Form II; then fresher; then nearer.
7.4 **Take the top‑ranked zone above and the top‑ranked below. No more than two live plans per day.**

---

### **Step 8 — The Approach and the Trigger**

Price arrives at `proximal_body`. Now:

8.1 **Grade A, fresh, `BIAS` aligned, arriving in a killzone:** a **limit order** at `proximal_body`
    is permitted. This is the only case in which anticipation is lawful.
8.2 **Grade B, or `taps = 1`, or arrival outside a killzone:** descend to **M5/M1** and require a
    **Change of Character** inside the rectangle:
   (a) price trades into `[proximal_wick, distal]`;
   (b) on M5 or M1, a swing point against the arrival is **closed through** (M1 CHoCH);
   (c) a fresh M1/M5 micro‑FVG is left by the reaction;
   (d) enter on the retest of that micro‑FVG or at market on the CHoCH close.
8.3 **Forbidden entries:** into a zone during a news block; against `BIAS` when `BIAS ≠ 0`; on the
    third tap; when the arrival candle into the zone is itself a displacement candle of `≥ 2A`
    (that is not a test, it is a breach in progress — **stand aside and wait for its base**).

---

### **Step 9 — The Stop (Invalidation Made Physical)**

9.1 `stop = distal ∓ buffer`, where
    `buffer = max(0.15 × A, $1.20) + spread`, and gold's spread is taken as **$0.30** unless measured otherwise.
9.2 The stop is **never** placed at the proximal, never at a round number, never at a fixed
    "20 pips." It is placed where the *hypothesis dies*, and the hypothesis dies beyond the distal.
9.3 If `stop distance > 0.8 × A_H4`, the zone is too coarse for day trading: **refine it on M5**
    (repeat Steps 2–4 on M5 within the M15 rectangle) or abandon it.

---

### **Step 10 — The Targets**

10.1 `R = |entry − stop|`.
10.2 **Gate of Reward:** the distance from entry to the **first** logical target must be `≥ 3R`.
     If it is not, the trade does not exist. Do not lower the target; delete the trade.
10.3 Targets, in order of priority:
   - **T1 (take 50%) = 1.5R**, or the nearest opposing Form IV imbalance (unfilled FVG), whichever is nearer.
   - **T2 (take 30%) =** the session's opposing liquidity: `AR_high`/`AR_low`, London high/low, `PDH`/`PDL`, or a cluster of equal highs/lows.
   - **T3 (runner, 20%) =** the nearest **unmitigated H4 opposing zone** (Step 1.4). Never beyond it.
10.4 On reaching T1, move stop to **breakeven + spread**. Trail thereafter beneath each new M5
     base that produces a `≥ 1.5A` displacement — never by a fixed distance.

---

### **Step 11 — The Governance of Risk (the Closed Ledger)**

11.1 Risk per trade: **0.5%** of equity (maximum 1.0% for a Grade A Form III in full HTF alignment).
11.2 Lot size for gold: `lots = (equity × risk%) / (stop_in_dollars × 100)`, since 1.00 lot = 100 oz
     and a $1.00 move = $100.00 per lot.
11.3 **Maximum two executed trades per day. After two consecutive losses, the ledger closes until
     the next session.** After a −2% day, it closes until the next week.
11.4 No new entries after **17:00 UTC**. All day‑trade positions flat by **20:00 UTC**.
11.5 One trade per zone. A zone that has taken your stop is never re‑entered on the same day.

---

### **Step 12 — The Record (without which the algorithm cannot improve)**

For every trade record: zone form (I/II/III), score and its eight components, session of birth,
session of execution, `A` at formation, taps at entry, entry mode (limit / CHoCH), `R` achieved,
and the maximum adverse excursion in units of `A`. After 100 trades, compute expectancy **by
score decile and by form**. Raise the gates on any decile whose expectancy is negative. **The
algorithm that is not measured is not an algorithm; it is a story.**

## VI. Proof of Superiority

### Geometric proof (the Rectangle of the Unfilled)

Consider the interval `[p, q]` traversed by price in a single displacement. Two propositions
about it are mutually exclusive:

- **(α)** The interval was traded *through* with two‑sided participation — then every price within
  it found both a buyer and a seller, the book was cleared, and nothing remains.
- **(β)** The interval was traversed by one‑sided aggression — then, of necessity, some resting
  intent at the origin was **not filled**, for the aggressor consumed all available counterparty
  and moved on. That unfilled intent has a location: the **base** from which the leg departed.

The **Fair Value Gap is the visible certificate of (β)**: it is the arithmetic proof that within
those three candles no two‑sided auction occurred, since the extremes did not overlap. Therefore:

> A zone accompanied by an FVG in its departure is a zone for which (β) is *proved*, not assumed.
> A zone without one is a zone for which (α) cannot be excluded.

This is why Step 3.4 is mandatory and non‑negotiable. It converts a belief into a demonstration.
No other method in common use requires this proof.

### Proof by exclusion — why the alternatives are shadows

1. **Classical Support/Resistance** marks *where price stopped*. But stopping is an **effect**;
   the unfilled order is the **cause**. To trade S/R is to trade the shadow and ignore the body
   that casts it. Worse, its central doctrine (more touches = stronger) is provably inverted:
   each touch consumes inventory. It is an equation that adds the very term it should subtract.
2. **Pivot points and Fibonacci** are arithmetic performed upon yesterday, containing **no
   reference to order flow whatsoever**. They are respected only when they coincide with a true
   zone — that is, they have no independent existence. A rule that works only when a second rule
   is present is not a rule; it is a coincidence.
3. **Unfiltered ICT / order‑block trading** identifies the correct *object* but omits the
   *magnitude gate*. Marking every last opposing candle produces a set so large that its members
   cannot be ranked, and an unranked set has the expectancy of its average member — which, after
   gold's spread, is negative. My Steps 3 and 6 are precisely the operators of **restoration**
   (removing false terms) and **balancing** (weighing like against like) applied to that set.
4. **Volume Profile alone** answers "where was volume transacted?" — that is, where the auction
   was **accepted**. But we seek where it was **rejected and left incomplete**. The High Volume
   Node is (α); we hunt (β). Volume profile is thus the correct instrument aimed at the wrong
   question. (Its Low Volume Nodes are useful — because they are FVGs measured by another means,
   which is my Step 3.4 arriving by a slower road. On XAUUSD, whose true volume is unknowable
   through fragmented spot feeds, that road is also an unreliable one.)
5. **Indicator confluence** re‑expresses past price as a second symbol and then treats the two
   symbols as independent evidence. By al‑muqābala, like terms on both sides cancel. Nothing is
   added; only confidence is inflated, which is the most expensive of all additions.

### Probabilistic proof — why conjunction, not accumulation

Let `P₀` be the base rate that price reacts at an arbitrary marked level (empirically near a coin
flip after costs). The eight criteria of the Mīzān are **not** redundant restatements of price;
each measures a distinct physical fact: **magnitude** (aggression), **tightness** (order
concentration), **inefficiency** (proof of one‑sidedness), **sweep** (deliberate manufacture of
counterparty), **HTF agreement** (direction of the dominant participant), **freshness**
(remaining inventory), **magazine proximity** (location of the fuel), **session** (presence of
the participant at all). Because they are near‑independent conditions on the same object, their
conjunction concentrates probability where mere accumulation of correlated indicators cannot.

This is exactly why the algorithm **discards below 65** rather than trading a weighted average.
The average of a large set regresses to `P₀`; only the **tail of the conjunction** carries edge.
The gates of Steps 2, 3, and 5 exist to make the set small. **A small set of proven zones is
worth more than a large set of plausible ones** — this is the whole doctrine in a sentence.

## VII. Specific Rules for XAUUSD Day Trading

Gold is not a currency pair and must not be treated as one. It is a metal quoted in a currency,
traded by hedgers, central banks, ETF desks, and futures arbitrageurs, and it possesses four
peculiarities that reshape every parameter above.

**1. Volatility and the units of measure.** Daily ATR commonly $25–$40; M15 ATR $2.50–$5.00.
Therefore **never use fixed pip stops**. Every distance in this book is expressed in units of `A`
so that the method is invariant under gold's volatility regimes. Spread is $0.20–$0.50 and widens
violently at 21:00–23:00 UTC and around news — hence Step 0.5 and Step 11.4.

**2. Round numbers as magazines, not walls.** Gold's participants place stops at `×10.00`,
`×25.00`, `×50.00`, `×100.00` with unusual discipline. The institutional behaviour is therefore
**to run the number and reverse from a zone just beyond it**. Consequence: a supply zone sitting
**$1–$4 above** a `×00.00` figure, or a demand zone **$1–$4 below** one, is the highest‑value
configuration on the instrument (Form III + criterion 7 together). A zone sitting *exactly on* the
number is inferior — the number is the bait, not the trap.

**3. Sessions (all UTC).**
   - **23:00–06:00 Asian — accumulation.** Mark `AR_high`, `AR_low`. **Do not trade the interior.**
     Typical Asian range on gold: $6–$14. If the range exceeds $18, the day is already trending —
     skip the Judas expectation and trade continuation zones only.
   - **06:00–07:00 — pre‑London.** Prepare; do not execute.
   - **07:00–10:00 London Judas.** The characteristic sequence: sweep of `AR_high` or `AR_low`
     → displacement back through the range → the base left behind is the **zone of the day**
     (Form III). If London instead displaces cleanly *without* a sweep, treat the base of that
     displacement as Form II and trade only with `BIAS`.
   - **10:00–12:30 — the lull.** No new zones are constructed; only management.
   - **12:30–16:00 New York.** Two states only: **continuation** (NY extends London's direction —
     trade London's fresh continuation zones on retrace) or **reversal** (NY sweeps the London
     high/low and displaces back — a second Form III, often the cleanest trade of the day,
     typically forming 13:30–15:00).
   - **After 17:00 — no new entries.** After 20:00 — flat. Overnight gold belongs to another game.

**4. Displacement and FVG are cleaner on gold than on FX.** Because gold moves in violent
impulses, the `2.0 × A` displacement gate is not a hardship — genuine institutional legs on gold
routinely displace $8–$25 on M15. If a candidate cannot clear `2.0 × A`, it is not institutional
activity; it is the market breathing.

**5. Practical parameter table (M15 construction):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Base length | 1–5 candles |
| Base height | ≤ 0.60 × A |
| Displacement | ≥ 2.0 × A from proximal |
| Strongest departure body/range | ≥ 0.60, opposing wick ≤ 25% |
| Minimum FVG width | ≥ 0.20 × A, absolute floor $0.60 |
| Freshness | 0 taps = A‑grade; 1 tap = B; 2 taps = dead |
| Entry | `proximal_body` (limit, Grade A) or M1/M5 CHoCH (Grade B) |
| Stop | `distal ∓ (max(0.15 × A, $1.20) + $0.30 spread)` |
| Minimum reward | 3R to first logical target |
| Risk | 0.5% (1.0% ceiling for Grade A Form III) |
| Daily limits | 2 trades; stop after 2 consecutive losses; no entries after 17:00 UTC |
| Invalidation | M15 close beyond distal, or 2nd tap, or session end, or news window |

**6. The forbidden days.** FOMC, CPI, NFP: do not construct zones before the release. The zones
built by the release itself (13:30 NY, after the first 15 minutes) are among the finest of the
month — they are Form III at maximum displacement — but they are traded **after** the block window,
never through it.

### Marginalia of the Scribe

The astrolabe does not command the stars; it only measures them. This book gives a decision
procedure of high rigour, not a promise of profit. Before a single dirham is risked, execute
Step 12 upon at least 200 historical occurrences of the instrument and satisfy yourself that the
expectancy is positive in *your* execution, with *your* spread, on *your* data. An algorithm
unverified is merely an opinion arranged in numbered lines.
