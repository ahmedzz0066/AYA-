## Buying Tips

1. **Always check multiple retailers** - Prices vary by 20-50 OMR for the same model across stores
2. **Look for seasonal sales** - Sharaf DG's back-to-school and Eid promotions can drop prices significantly
3. **Verify RAM amount** - Some listings show inflated specs; confirm 8 GB or 16 GB at checkout
4. **SSD is non-negotiable** - All options above include SSD; avoid any laptop still shipping with HDD-only storage
5. **Check warranty terms** - Dell and HP typically offer the best local warranty service in Oman
6. **Consider RAM upgradability** - If buying an 8 GB model, check if it has an extra RAM slot for future upgrades
